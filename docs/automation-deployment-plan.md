Both production configs keep `DEMO_MODE="true"`, the same business, D1 database, and allowed origin. The proposed model is `@cf/meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct-fp8-fast`, which Cloudflare lists as active, JSON-schema capable, and available within its Free Workers AI allocation. The code caps each model response at 160 tokens, validates every structured result, and falls back to deterministic rules or drafts on invalid output, timeout, or quota failure. Visitor enquiry and lead text reaches the model only after explicit AI consent; contact strings are redacted on a best-effort basis. Model output cannot book, confirm, approve, change prices, send messages, or bypass permission checks. Owner price changes and all customer-facing drafts still require human approval.

The Pages `AI` binding is declared only for its `production` environment. Local Pages, CI, and preview paths have no AI binding and make no remote inference calls. The private Cron Worker has a production-only config file. Do not add `AUTOMATION_TICK_TOKEN`, `HUBSPOT_SERVICE_KEY`, external `LLM_*` secrets, messaging credentials, R2, KV, Queues, a paid model, a new Worker, or a custom domain. CRM remains mock-only and notification delivery remains a mock outbox. No real customer contact or CRM data is sent to external providers in this demo.
